# Mobile Experience - Aurora Hotel

## Confirmed priority journeys
- Availability search from the homepage
- Date range and guest selection
- Room and rate-plan comparison
- Three-step multi-room booking checkout
- Payment status and booking confirmation
- Booking lookup, management, and cancellation
- Receptionist check-in and check-out
- Housekeeping room-status updates

## Catalog-derived floors
- Compose priority journeys for mobile; do not merely shrink desktop layout.
- Preserve visible focus, readable type, reduced motion, and 44-by-44-pixel
  internal tap-target guidance under 768px.
- Prevent horizontal overflow at 320, 375, 414, and 768 pixels.
